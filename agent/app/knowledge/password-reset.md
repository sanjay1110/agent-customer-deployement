# Resetting your password

1. Go to the Acme Cloud Storage sign-in page and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account. A reset link is sent within 5 minutes.
3. The link expires after 30 minutes. If it expired, request a new one.
4. Accounts with single sign-on (SSO) cannot reset passwords here; contact your company IT administrator.

If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder and confirm the address is correct.
