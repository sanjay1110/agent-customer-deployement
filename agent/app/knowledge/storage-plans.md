# Storage plans and limits

| Plan | Storage | Max file size | Version history |
|------|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Basic | 100 GB | 5 GB | 30 days |
| Team | 1 TB per user | 50 GB | 180 days |
| Enterprise | Custom | 250 GB | 1 year |

Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle,
and you must be under the new plan's storage limit before downgrading.
